UFC Freedom Fight 250 Odds: Topuria vs. Gaethje

Written by: Garrett Kerman

Sunday, June 14, 2026, is circled on every fight fan's calendar, and for good reason. UFC Freedom 250 arrives as one of the most unique events in the promotion's history, a seven-fight main card hosted on the South Lawn of the White House in Washington, D.C., live on Paramount+ at 8 p.m. ET. Coinciding with Flag Day and President Trump's 80th birthday as part of America's 250th anniversary celebrations, this is not your average fight night. Two title fights, 12 ranked fighters, and a setting unlike anything the sport has ever seen, this card demands your full attention. Here is a full fight-by-fight betting breakdown of everything you need to know heading into Sunday night.

Ilia Topuria vs. Justin Gaethje: UFC Lightweight Championship

The main event is a title unification bout between undefeated lightweight champion Ilia Topuria (17-0) and interim titleholder Justin Gaethje (27-5), who earned his shot by beating Paddy Pimblett. Topuria carries an 88% finish rate into the fight, while Gaethje's sits at 77% over a far larger sample size. The Georgian sniper has already brutally knocked out Alexander Volkanovski and Max Holloway; the question is whether the 37-year-old Gaethje has enough speed and durability left to survive the early rounds.

The prediction markets are overwhelming: Polymarket has Topuria at 81% and Kalshi puts him at 80% to win. A KO/TKO prop for Topuria is listed at -210 on most books, reasonable as a parlay leg, and Topuria in Round 1 sits around +270 for those hunting plus money.

Alex Pereira vs. Ciryl Gane: UFC Interim Heavyweight Championship

This is the most compelling betting matchup on the card from a pure value standpoint. Pereira (13-3) is making his heavyweight debut seeking to become the first-ever three-division UFC champion, having already held belts at middleweight and light heavyweight. Gane (13-2), the decorated heavyweight technician, holds the natural physical and experience advantages at 265 pounds.
